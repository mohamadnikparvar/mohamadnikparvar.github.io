Warehouse Management System: from scope to prototype

Product · WMS · In-house product · 2026

  • 77 features mapped in 8 areas
  • 15 screens in the prototype
  • 3 months to the WMS MVP
  1. Mapped the full scope with stakeholders into 8 areas and 77 features, from warehouse setup to mobile (PDA) operations and dashboards.
  2. Designed multi-stage receipts and deliveries with quality control, quantity control, putaway and picking.
  3. Added location and item-based physical counts with manager and finance approval.
  4. Built a clickable prototype with 15 screens, including live tank monitoring with sensors and alerts, to validate flows with users before development.

Research · Master's thesis

What really drives a successful ERP rollout?

Many ERP projects miss their goals because teams focus on the technology and neglect management, organization and people. I surveyed managers, specialists and ERP users at Shoniz to find which drivers actually predict a successful rollout, and by how much.

A Model of the Drivers of ERP Implementation: a case study at Shoniz (Dadash Baradar Industrial Co.)

Top-management support is necessary, but not enough. Its effect only shows up when it turns into execution: a capable team, budget control, user training and solid infrastructure.

  • 250 managers and users surveyed
  • 48 → 18 questions refined by experts and factor analysis
  • 0.95 reliability (Cronbach's alpha)
  • 53% of rollout success explained (R²)
